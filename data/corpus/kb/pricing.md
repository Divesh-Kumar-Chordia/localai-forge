# Acme Cloud Pricing and Plans

Acme Cloud is a file storage, sync and collaboration service for teams. Prices are per user per month in USD unless stated otherwise.

## Plans

### Starter
- Price: free for up to 3 users.
- Storage: 5 GB per user.
- Version history: 7 days.
- Support: community forum only.

### Pro
- Price: $12 per user per month billed monthly, or $9.60 per user per month billed annually.
- Storage: 1 TB per user.
- Version history: 90 days.
- Support: email support with a 24-hour first-response target.
- Minimum 2 users.

### Business
- Price: $25 per user per month billed monthly, or $20 per user per month billed annually.
- Storage: 5 TB per user, pooled across the team.
- Version history: 1 year.
- Includes SSO (SAML 2.0 and OIDC), audit logs, and admin roles.
- Support: priority email and chat with a 4-hour first-response target.
- Minimum 5 users.

### Enterprise
- Price: custom; contact sales. Typical list starts at $40 per user per month with a 100-seat minimum.
- Storage: unlimited (fair use).
- Version history: unlimited.
- Includes everything in Business plus data residency selection (US, EU, India), customer-managed encryption keys (BYOK), a 99.95% uptime SLA, and a named technical account manager.
- Support: 24/7 phone support with a 1-hour first-response target for Severity 1 incidents.

## Annual billing discount
Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing on Pro and Business. Annual plans are invoiced up front for 12 months.

## Storage overage
If a team exceeds its pooled storage, uploads are not blocked for 14 days. After the grace period, additional storage is billed at $0.05 per GB per month, or uploads are paused until usage is reduced.

## Adding and removing seats
Seats added mid-cycle are prorated to the day. Seats removed mid-cycle on monthly plans take effect at the next billing date. On annual plans, seats can be added at any time (prorated) but can only be reduced at renewal.

## Taxes
Prices exclude VAT, GST and sales tax, which are added at checkout based on the billing address.
