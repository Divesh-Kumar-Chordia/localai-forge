# Data Export and Retention

## Exporting your data
Admins on any paid plan can export all workspace data from Admin Console > Data > Export. Exports include files, folder structure and sharing permissions as a JSON manifest. Audit logs can be exported as CSV on Business and Enterprise. Large exports are delivered as multiple 50 GB ZIP parts; a download link is emailed to the requesting admin and expires after 7 days.

## Retention after cancellation
After a subscription is cancelled, data is retained in read-only mode for 90 days and then permanently deleted from primary storage. Backups containing the data are purged within a further 35 days, so deletion is complete within 125 days of cancellation.

## Deleted files
Deleted files go to the Trash and can be restored for the length of the plan's version history (7 days on Starter, 90 days on Pro, 1 year on Business, unlimited on Enterprise). Admins can permanently purge the Trash at any time.

## Legal hold
Enterprise customers can place users or folders on legal hold, which prevents permanent deletion regardless of retention settings until the hold is released.

## Data subject requests
For GDPR or DPDP Act requests, customers acting as data controllers can use the export and delete tools above. Acme Cloud, acting as processor, will assist with requests within 10 business days.
