# Refunds, Cancellation and Pausing

## Refund policy
- Monthly plans: you can request a full refund within 7 days of the first charge. After 7 days, monthly charges are non-refundable, but you can cancel to stop future charges.
- Annual plans: you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis for unused full months, minus a 10% early-termination fee.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
- Storage overage charges and Enterprise professional-services fees are non-refundable.

## How to cancel
Account owners can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and the workspace becomes read-only. Files remain downloadable for 90 days after cancellation, after which they are permanently deleted (see Data Export and Retention).

## Pausing instead of cancelling
Teams on monthly Pro or Business plans can pause their subscription for up to 3 months once per year. While paused, the workspace is read-only, no charges are made, and all data is retained. Pausing is not available on annual plans or Enterprise.

## Do not cancel if you only want to downgrade
If you only want fewer features, downgrade instead of cancelling: Settings > Billing > Change plan. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date and keep all your data, as long as usage fits within the new plan's storage limit.

## Reactivation
A cancelled workspace can be reactivated within 90 days by an account owner. After 90 days the data is gone and a new workspace must be created.
