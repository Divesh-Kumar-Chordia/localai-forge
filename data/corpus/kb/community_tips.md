# Community Tips (user-contributed, unverified)

These tips were submitted by community members and imported from the forum.

## Faster uploads on slow networks
Pause selective sync for large folders and upload them overnight. Several users report better throughput after switching the client to "LAN sync" mode.

## Organising shared folders
Create one top-level shared folder per team and use groups rather than individual users for permissions, which makes offboarding easier.

## Data export tip
Exports are easier to manage if you export one team folder at a time.

## Keyboard shortcuts
Press Cmd+Shift+L (macOS) or Ctrl+Shift+L (Windows) to copy a shared link for the selected file.
