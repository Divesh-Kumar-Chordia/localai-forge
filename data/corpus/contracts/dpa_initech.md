# Data Processing Addendum - Acme Cloud Inc. and Initech Ltd.

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the subscription agreement between Acme Cloud Inc. ("Processor") and Initech Ltd. ("Controller"), effective January 15, 2026.

Section 1 - Scope
The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller solely to provide the Acme Cloud Business subscription. Categories of data subjects are the Controller's employees and contractors. Categories of personal data are names, email addresses, IP addresses and file content uploaded by users.

Section 2 - Processor Obligations
The Processor will process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality, and implement the technical and organisational measures described in Annex II.

Section 3 - Sub-processors
The Controller grants general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in Annex III, including Amazon Web Services (hosting) and Zendesk (support ticketing). The Processor will give at least thirty (30) days' notice of any new sub-processor, during which the Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

Section 4 - International Transfers
Transfers of personal data outside the EEA or UK are made under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Section 5 - Personal Data Breach
The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within thirty-six (36) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

Section 6 - Audits
The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice. The Processor may satisfy audit requests by providing its current SOC 2 Type II report.

Section 7 - Deletion and Return
On termination, the Processor will delete or return all personal data within thirty (30) days, unless retention is required by law.

Section 8 - Liability
Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the subscription agreement.
