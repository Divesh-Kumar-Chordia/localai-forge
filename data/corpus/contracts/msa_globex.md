# Master Services Agreement - Acme Cloud Inc. and Globex Corporation

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 ("Effective Date") between Acme Cloud Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and Globex Corporation, an Ohio corporation ("Customer").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means the Acme Cloud Enterprise subscription described in the Order Form.
1.2 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under each Order Form.
1.3 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential.

2. Term and Renewal
2.1 Initial Term. The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.
2.2 Renewal. The Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
2.3 Price Increases. Provider may increase Fees upon renewal by no more than five percent (5%) over the prior term's Fees, with at least ninety (90) days' written notice.

3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Customer will pay Fees annually in advance. Invoices are due net forty-five (45) days from the invoice date.
3.2 Late amounts accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.
3.3 Provider may suspend the Services if any undisputed amount is more than thirty (30) days overdue after written notice.

4. Service Levels
4.1 Availability. Provider will make the Services available 99.95% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.
4.2 Service Credits. If monthly availability falls below 99.95%, Customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's Fees; below 99.0%, 25%; below 95.0%, 50%.
4.3 Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures, and must be requested within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month.

5. Data Protection
5.1 Provider will process Customer personal data only in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum.
5.2 Provider will store Customer content in the European Union region (Frankfurt) as selected by Customer.
5.3 Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed security breach affecting Customer data within forty-eight (48) hours.

6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information using at least reasonable care and will not disclose it except to employees and contractors with a need to know.
6.2 Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years after termination.

7. Warranties
7.1 Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the documentation.
7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES.

8. Indemnification
8.1 Provider will defend Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Services infringe intellectual property rights, and pay resulting damages finally awarded.
8.2 Customer will defend Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer content.

9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 Exclusion. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or lost profits.
9.2 Cap. Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
9.3 Exceptions. The cap in Section 9.2 does not apply to breaches of Section 6 (Confidentiality), a party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, or a party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct. For breaches of Section 5 (Data Protection), liability is capped at two times (2x) the Fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

10. Termination
10.1 For Convenience. Customer may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice, but Fees for the remainder of the then-current term remain payable.
10.2 For Cause. Either party may terminate if the other materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice.
10.3 Effect. Upon termination, Provider will make Customer content available for export for sixty (60) days, then delete it.

11. Governing Law
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in New York County.

12. General
12.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to a successor in a merger or acquisition.
12.2 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the Order Form.
